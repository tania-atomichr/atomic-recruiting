# Gold-standard example (the tone AND the word register to imitate)

This is Tania's own reference post. It is the target: punchy, marketing-forward, specific, active, bullet-driven, professional, and written in **normal words a competent professional would actually use**. Imitate the rhythm, the specificity, AND the plain vocabulary. Only two house rules are applied on top of her raw example: **no em/en dashes** (use commas, colons, "to", or restructure) and **compensation as a monthly USD market estimate plus an invite to share their range** (her raw example quoted hourly; we quote monthly + invite). ## Word register — DO NOT "upgrade" these words
Her words are normal and professional. Keep them. Snappier substitutions are what make copy sound buzzy and fake:
- "identify" — NOT "spot"
- "convert" / "translate" (feedback into requirements) — NOT "turn"
- "documentation" / "help documentation" — NOT "docs"
- "release" (a feature) — NOT "ship"
- "improve productivity" — NOT "work faster"
- "applications" — fine; "mobile applications" — do not force it to "apps" for pace

**Title:** Product Specialist | Property Management SaaS 🚀🏠

**Hook (pitch, must be ≤ 200 chars):** Turn real customer feedback into better product decisions. You'll shape requirements, test features through real-world field workflows, and help a profitable SaaS company release reliable tools used by property professionals.

*Apply for this job*

**Company Overview**

Our client is a bootstrapped, profitable U.S.-based SaaS company building inspection and documentation software for property managers. Founded in 2014, its platform supports 20,000+ active users across the U.S. and more than 20 countries, with over 100 million inspection photos processed.

Its mobile and web applications help teams capture geotagged photos, videos, and voice notes; work offline; generate reports; assign tasks; and connect inspection data with established property management systems. The company is also applying AI to turn field voice notes into structured inspection reports.

**Your Role**

As Product Specialist, you'll bring the customer's perspective into every stage of the product cycle, from early discovery and requirements to hands-on testing and release readiness.

Reporting to the Head of Engineering, you'll work closely with Customer Success, Support, and Marketing to ensure the team understands the right problems, builds practical solutions, and releases features that behave predictably in real inspection workflows.

Success means clearer engineering inputs, fewer post-release surprises, faster resolution of recurring issues, and well-documented product decisions.

**You'll:**
- Analyze customer feedback and identify recurring workflow problems
- Translate messy input into clear, actionable product requirements
- Document edge cases, tradeoffs, dependencies, and open questions
- Test features across complete mobile and web workflows
- Reproduce customer issues and verify their root causes
- Confirm fixes solve the original problem, not just symptoms
- Coordinate accurate release notes with Marketing and Support
- Surface product risks and blockers before they affect delivery

**You Bring:**
- 3+ years in product, QA, implementation, or SaaS operations
- Experience converting customer feedback into structured requirements
- Strong hands-on software testing and edge-case thinking
- Excellent written and spoken English communication
- Clear, concise, and well-organized documentation habits
- A process-oriented, self-directed approach to ownership
- Confidence collaborating across technical and customer-facing teams
- Practical experience using AI tools to improve productivity

**Bonus Points:**
- Property management, field services, or inspection experience
- Experience writing release notes, FAQs, or help documentation
- Familiarity with issue trackers and product analytics tools
- Exposure to mobile applications, offline workflows, or integrations

**What's Offered:**
- Fully remote contractor role across the Americas
- Approximately 40 hours per week, with some flexibility
- What we'd estimate for a role like this: around $2,800 to $4,100 USD per month, based on experience, impact, location, and hours
- Paid weekly in USD. Share the range that fits your level and we'll go from there
- Up to 20 paid days off per year
- Direct collaboration with the Head of Engineering
- Meaningful influence over product quality and customer experience

This role requires reliable overlap with the company's primary working window of 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Pacific Time.

**Interview Process:**
1️⃣ **Intro Call:** Discuss your background, motivation, and product approach
2️⃣ **Practical Exercise:** Analyze a workflow and draft clear requirements

## Why this works (the checklist)
- **Hook is active and concrete,** not "We are seeking a candidate who..."
- **Company Overview is specific and credible** (founding year, user count, countries, photo volume), anonymized, and kept as TWO short paragraphs.
- **Your Role is short paragraphs, active voice,** ending in a plain "Success means..." line from the brief's own indicators.
- **Bullets are tight, verb-first, and written in normal words** (identify, translate, document, coordinate), roughly 5 to 8 per section.
- **Offer is concrete:** hours, monthly market estimate plus invite, PTO, who they report to, the working window.
- **No em-dashes, no rule-of-three flourishes, no hype words, and no snappy word-substitutions.** The punch is in the specifics and the structure.
