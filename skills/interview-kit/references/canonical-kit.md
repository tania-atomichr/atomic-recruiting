## The flow (as of 2026-07-07 evening, Tania-approved structure)
1. **Intro - 2** (#70311) — safe, friendly, exploratory tone.
2. **★ General Background & Career Path** (#102696) — THE canonical story slot. PURE OPENER only (Tania, final): "quick overview of your background / how did you get started / what roles and industries" — nothing else. Everything that ever lived here moved out: transitions → #63097, company types/agency → #212257, industries/customers → #212258, education → #212256, "enjoyed most" → Signals #212233, "explore next" → Motivation. The shifted-priorities/ambiguity line was DROPPED entirely (Tania: doesn't uncover anything not already covered) — working-practices has no dedicated ask by design. Do not re-add follow-ups here; one home per signal.
3. **Transitions | Optional ✳️** (#63097) — separate slot ON PURPOSE (recruiters forget follow-ups); marked optional, skip when the story covered it.
3b. **Company Environments | Optional ✳️** (#212257, skill: 06. Environment) — recent roles: product vs agency/consulting vs enterprise, startup vs corporate — the recency-weighted axis the fit score grades. Optional: skip if the story already made it clear.
3c. **Business & Customers | Optional ✳️** (#212258, skill: 07. Domain) — REWRITTEN 2026-07-07 to Tania's conversational layered shape: one open ask ("how would you describe your most recent company in your own words — what did it actually do?") + pause, then casual only-if-not-covered follow-ups (who used it vs who paid / their world & what got in the way / surprising uses). NO explicit verticals ask — seniors include markets unprompted, juniors omitting them is itself a signal; recruiter notes it. SECOND-CHANNEL READ (Tania): HOW they answer is itself a signal — crisp explanation of customer + money flow = business mindset / customer empathy / product understanding; a mid/senior who can't answer is a flag (junior not knowing is normal). Note the clarity, never correct or challenge on the call. Skip condition = the CANDIDATE explained it themselves (unprompted = great signal); the recruiter knowing from research doesn't count. Signals #212233 opening ask is Tania's layered phrasing ("what kind of work have you enjoyed the most? any project you feel proud of?") — seniors take it as show-off room, juniors as work-they-liked; both valid.
4. **Scope of Work** (#176889) — generic ownership patterns: what their role usually included, where responsibilities started and ended. (#70390 = "Scope of Work | Engineering", dev kits only.)
5. **★ Signals of Strength | Optional ✳️** (#212233) — the show-off piece, what people come to them for, mentoring (senior+), and the EXPLICIT portfolio/work-samples ask ("anything we could actually look at — it helps when we present you"). Optional: often answered along the way; ask only what's missing. Rewritten 2026-07-07 per Tania (old wording sounded fake).
6. **← TAILORED DOMAINS insert here** (Core skills section in the layout). Keep Curiosity/AI alongside any AI-flavored domain — different purposes; do NOT drop #152252 as "superseded".
6b. **Education & Learning | Optional ✳️** (#212256, skill: 09. Education) — what they studied/trained in formally + certifications (feeds the fit-score's field-relevance grading), then the taught-yourself-recently read. Sits right before Curiosity/AI (the learning pair). Optional: skip if study/certs already covered — the optional marker means "skip if organically covered", the evidence itself is still required for the rating.
7. **Curiosity / AI** (#152252) — full script includes the AI-tools-in-daily-work chain and "how do you keep quality high when AI is in the loop". Edited 2026-07-07: examples generalized from dev-only to role-agnostic (writing, research, reporting, data tasks, scheduling, automating repetitive work — or coding, if that's your world).
8. **Motivation 2** (#82970) — extended: what they want, what would make it the right move, where they are in their search. **Industry affinity is read HERE, unprompted — never ask an industry-interest question directly (naming the sector biases the candidate to perform interest; Tania's rule). #63978/#67862 are NOT picks anymore.** Bonus skill nice-to-haves live softly inside the relevant domain, or #70399.
9. **Availability & Salary Expectations** (#63103) — the Logistics section; keep the full chain.
10. **★ Wrap-Up** (#212234) — NOT "what questions do you have" (they've asked along the way + received the role doc). Three beats: anything-we-didn't-ask, transparent NEXT STEPS ("I'll prepare my notes from this call and submit your profile; as soon as we have feedback you'll hear from me — either way"), and CONNECT ON LINKEDIN **LIVE, before hanging up** — the recruiter sends the invite during the call and waits for the accept (emails go unresponsive/spam; LinkedIn is the guaranteed second channel — Tania's rule). Rewritten 2026-07-07 per Tania.
11. **Go / Maybe / No** (#82972) — always last.

## Layout recipe (competence_order) — sections only, since 2026-07-07 EVERY question carries a skill
Tania's rule: **all questions have a skill/trait assigned** (verified 0 untagged across live kits). So the layout is pure section ordering — questions render inside their skill's section, ordered by row_order. **Build rule: read the Template's LIVE competence_order and insert `{67334 Core skills ← the Tailored Domains}` immediately AFTER the `{67332 Attitude}` section (Signals of Strength) and before `{67340 Education}`.** Current full order for reference:

## Scorecard picks (Skills & traits selector)
The Template and every new kit select **all 11 areas + Conclusion = 12 picks** (every area visible and ratable — verified across all 38 live kits 2026-07-07). Education now has a dedicated question: **Education & Learning (#212256)**, placed before Curiosity/AI. It exists because the fit-score rubric grades Education on **field relevance + required certifications** — evidence that won't surface reliably from the story alone — plus the keep-learning read. Environment (#212257) and Domain (#212258) got the same treatment 2026-07-07 after reading their rubric dimensions: Environment grades product-vs-agency-vs-enterprise on RECENT roles; Domain grades business context (customer + how the money flows). Every area now has a dedicated question except Communication (the call itself is the test) and Core skills (the role's Tailored Domains).

**The dedicated-question principle:** a skill gets its own question when the FIT-SCORE RUBRIC's evidence for it won't surface reliably anywhere else — never one-question-per-skill as a quota. When designing or reviewing canonical questions, read the corresponding dimension in `~/.claude/skills/candidate-fit-score/RUBRIC.md` first: the interview should collect exactly the evidence the scorer grades.
